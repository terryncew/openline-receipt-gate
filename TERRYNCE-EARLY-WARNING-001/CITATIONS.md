## Recovery as an early-warning quantity

van Belzen, J. et al. (2017).
**Vegetation recovery in tidal marshes reveals critical slowing down under increased inundation.**
Nature Communications 8, 15811.
DOI: 10.1038/ncomms15811

This is prior art for direct recovery rate as a resilience/early-warning observable.
TERRYNCE-EARLY-WARNING-001 does not claim novelty for critical slowing down. Its narrower
test is whether a pre-relief, lag-aware signed recoverability margin adds held-out
information beyond state/trend/history/resilience baselines.
