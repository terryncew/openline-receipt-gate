# External confirmation handoff

The Jain panel is exhausted as a discovery set. The frozen continuous selector may not be tuned further on it.

A candidate external panel must be screened without running the selector first. Before outcome scoring, freeze: candidate inclusion, assay mapping, liability thresholds, unit-cost or real-cost definition, missingness policy, and the information-gain comparator.

A strong currently identified candidate is the 2025 Ginkgo/Arsiwala PROPHET-Ab dataset (246 IgGs; 10 developability assays in the published supplemental file). It includes newly generated high-throughput measurements and overlapping assay families such as HIC, SMAC and AC-SINS, but it also overlaps historically with the Jain clinical-antibody universe. Therefore a confirmation protocol must quantify identity overlap and should prefer antibodies not present in Jain for the primary external analysis.

The frozen selector earns an external allocation claim only if it improves assay-to-first-liability efficiency without worsening false reassurance against the strongest admissible baseline. If not, disposition is EXTERNAL_GENERALIZATION_NOT_SUPPORTED.
